Symbols

x, u, w — state, control, random noise

f, g, gN — model, stage cost, final cost

N, ℓ — horizon, lookahead steps

xk+ℓ|k — prediction of time k+ℓ made at k

Q, R, QF — state, input, terminal weights

U, X — stacked future inputs, predictions

Φ, Λ, Qblk — batch matrices, stacked weights

Sk, Lk, K — Riccati matrix, feedback gains

Jk(x), J* — cost-to-go, optimal cost

μ, π, μ* — policy, policy sequence, optimal

pij(u) — probability i → j under u

α — discount factor, 0 ≤ α < 1

T, Tμ, Vk — Bellman operators, VI iterate

J~, φ, r — approx. cost, features, weights

β, γ — training target, step size

Q(x,u) — Q-factor (action value)

δ, ε — learning rate, exploration prob.

Γ, σ² — delivery prob., noise variance

κ(x) — MPC feedback law
